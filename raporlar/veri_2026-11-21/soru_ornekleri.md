# Soru örnekleri (iki test)

Kaynak: sunucu deposundaki soru dosyaları (canlıdaki havuzun kaynağı). Doğru şık kalın yazıldı. Her seviyeden 5'er örnek.

## "Seviyeni yeniden ölç": boşluk doldurma parçaları

Her parça üç boşluk; kullanıcı her boşluk için üç şıktan birini seçer.

### A1 (6 parça)

> Ali gets up at six every day. He drinks a glass of ___. Then he walks to the stop on the corner. The ___ comes at seven. He reads a book on it. Nobody talks. He likes the quiet ___ to work.

1. **milk** · bread · rice
2. shop · **bus** · park
3. milk · bed · **ride**

> Mia has a small cat. The cat is white and ___. It sleeps on the sofa all day. In the evening it ___ with a ball. Mia gives it food at eight, because then it is ___.

4. from · **grey** · very
5. reads · cooks · **plays**

### A2 (6 parça)

> Yesterday Deniz lost the ___ of his flat. He looked for it in his bag and in his coat. Then he remembered the cafe near the office. He went back and ___ the waiter about it. The waiter smiled and gave it to him. Deniz was very ___ to have it back.

1. **key** · bill · map
2. hugged · **asked** · phoned
3. late · tired · **glad**

> Last summer we travelled to a small town by the ___. The weather was bad on the first day, so we ___ in the hotel. On the second day the sun came out and we walked along the beach. We ate fish at a little restaurant and talked for hours. We will go there again next year, because the town was quiet and ___.

4. hill · **sea** · road
5. cooked · danced · **stayed**

### B1 (6 parça)

> The bookshop on the corner has been open since my grandfather was a boy. Its ___, who knows every customer by name, keeps a list of the books they love. If you ask him for a ___, he will disappear behind the shelves for a minute. He always comes back with something you did not know you ___.

1. **owner** · reader · author
2. explanation · **suggestion** · translation
3. ordered · returned · **wanted**

> When Selin moved to a new city, she had not expected the ___ of the evenings. She had lived in a noisy street for years, and she ___ the sound of traffic. If she had known, she would have chosen a flat near the centre. After a few months, however, the quiet became the thing she liked ___.

4. comfort · **silence** · expense
5. hated · feared · **missed**

### B2 (6 parça)

> The council's decision to ban late-night deliveries was announced as a ___ for residents, and for a few weeks it felt like one. Streets that had rattled with lorries at three in the morning fell silent. What the announcement did not mention was that the same deliveries would now arrive at seven, ___ when the school run began. Complaints did not fall; they simply changed ___.

1. **victory** · warning · proposal
2. similarly · **precisely** · generally
3. opinion · purpose · **address**

> It was widely ___ that handwriting would disappear within a generation, and in offices it very nearly has. Yet stationery shops report that sales of notebooks have risen for six ___ years, driven not by older customers but by people in their twenties. Asked why, they rarely mention nostalgia. More often they describe something closer to ___: a page that does not update, notify, or suggest what to write next.

4. forgotten · **predicted** · concluded
5. challenging · predictable · **consecutive**

## "Seviyeni belgele": dört şıklı sorular

Sıra: ısınma (6 soru, karışık seviye) → üç metin (4'er soru) → kontrol (en çok 4 soru).

### A1

**Isınma soruları (bu seviyeden iki örnek):**

1. Sign: "Shop open 9:00–17:00, Monday to Saturday." When is the shop closed?
   **On Sunday** · On Monday · On Friday · On Saturday
2. Note: "Mum, I'm at Ben's house. I'm home at six. Love, Sam." Where is Sam now?
   At his home · **At Ben's house** · At his school · At the station

**Metin (message):**

> Hi Tom, I am at the shop now. I want to make a cake tonight. Do we have eggs at home? I can see milk and sugar here, but I can't find butter. Please call me. Thanks, Anna

3. Why does Anna write to Tom?
   **To ask about eggs at home** · To invite him to her party · To say that she is ill · To tell him about a film
4. What can't Anna find in the shop?
   Milk · **Butter** · Sugar · Coffee

**Kontrol sorusu:**

5. "My brother is twelve. I am ten." Who is older?
   The writer · Both are ten · Both are twelve · **The brother**

### A2

**Isınma soruları (bu seviyeden iki örnek):**

1. "Sorry, I missed the train, so I'll be about twenty minutes late. Start without me!" What does the writer want?
   **The others should start now** · The others should wait for her · The meeting should stop · Someone should call her
2. "We've sold all the tickets for Saturday. There are a few left for Sunday." What can you do?
   Buy a ticket for Saturday · **Buy a ticket for Sunday** · Get a free ticket · Buy tickets for both days

**Metin (email):**

> Hi Jess, thanks for your email. I'm sorry I didn't answer sooner, but we moved to the new flat last week and the internet only started working yesterday. The flat is smaller than our old one, but it's much closer to my office, so I can walk to work now. There's a nice bakery downstairs too, which is dangerous! Are you free next Saturday? Come for lunch and see the place. Love, Mia

3. What is Mia's email mainly about?
   **Her new home and an invitation** · A problem with her new job · A holiday she wants to take with Jess · A birthday party for Jess
4. Why didn't Mia answer sooner?
   She was ill all week · **Her internet did not work** · She was on holiday · She lost Jess's email address

**Kontrol sorusu:**

5. "Ben usually walks to work, but today he took the bus because of the rain." Why did Ben take the bus?
   He was late for work · His car was broken · **The weather was bad** · He moved house

### B1

**Isınma soruları (bu seviyeden iki örnek):**

1. "I'd love to come to your party, but I've promised to help my brother move house that weekend." What will the writer do?
   Go to the party · **Help her brother** · Move to a new house · Have a party at home
2. "The concert was postponed because the singer was ill. It will now take place next month." "Postponed" means …
   cancelled for ever · stopped in the middle · **moved to a later date** · moved to a new city

**Metin (article):**

> Several companies in Iceland tried a four-day working week between 2015 and 2019. About 2,500 workers took part. They worked fewer hours, usually 35 or 36 instead of 40, but their pay stayed the same. Researchers wanted to know whether the workers would get less done. The results surprised many managers. In most workplaces, the amount of work stayed the same, and in some it even went up. Workers said they felt less stressed and had more time for their families and hobbies. One reason was that teams cut long meetings and stopped doing tasks that were not really necessary. After the trial, unions in Iceland agreed new contracts, and today a large number of workers have the right to shorter hours. Other countries, including Spain and the UK, have since started their own trials.

3. What is the text mainly about?
   **A trial of shorter working weeks** · Why managers in Iceland earn more · How families spend their weekends · A plan to close offices on Fridays
4. How many hours did the workers usually work during the trial?
   40 hours · **35 or 36 hours** · 45 hours · 25 or 30 hours

**Kontrol sorusu:**

5. "The 8:15 train to Leeds will leave from platform 6 instead of platform 2 today." Where should passengers go?
   Platform 2 · Platform 8 · Platform 15 · **Platform 6**

### B2

**Isınma soruları (bu seviyeden iki örnek):**

1. "Had I known the traffic would be this bad, I would have taken the train." What does the speaker mean?
   **They drove and regret it** · They took the train · They prefer driving to trains · The train was also delayed
2. "The new manager was reluctant to change the schedule, but eventually agreed after several complaints." "Reluctant" means …
   eager · **unwilling** · unable · frightened

**Metin (article):**

> Most people assume that putting things off is a problem of laziness or poor time management. Recent research suggests something different: procrastination is largely about emotions. When a task makes us feel anxious, bored or insecure, avoiding it gives us immediate relief, even though we know the delay will cost us later. In other words, we are not avoiding the work itself so much as the unpleasant feelings attached to it. This explains why apps and planners often fail. A detailed schedule does nothing to reduce the fear of writing a weak report. What seems to work better is lowering the emotional cost of starting. Psychologists recommend breaking a task into a first step so small that it feels almost silly, such as opening the document and writing one sentence. Another approach is self-compassion. In one study, students who forgave themselves for procrastinating before their first exam procrastinated less before the next one, while those who criticised themselves harshly continued the pattern. Being kind to yourself, it turns out, is not the same as letting yourself off the hook; it removes one of the bad feelings that made you delay in the first place.

3. What is the main argument of the text?
   **Procrastination is mainly an emotional problem** · Good planning apps can end procrastination · Students who are lazy usually fail their exams · Most people manage their time very badly
4. Why, according to the text, do planners often fail?
   They take too much time to set up properly · **They do not address the feelings involved** · People forget to look at them · They make tasks seem too large

**Kontrol sorusu:**

5. "Despite months of rehearsal, the opening night was a disaster; the lead actor forgot his lines twice." What can we infer?
   The actors had not rehearsed · **Preparation did not prevent problems** · The play was a great success · The audience enjoyed the mistakes very much
